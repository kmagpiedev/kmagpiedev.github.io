# Art direction

## Climax reference

`assets/climax-art-direction.png` is a non-runtime concept reference generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 2026-08-28. The WebGL renderer does not display or sample this bitmap; it is kept beside the source as a composition and color target.

## Generation brief

- Use case: stylized-concept.
- Format: 16:9 keyframe for a public LED media-art film.
- Subject: a bright vertical Milky Way, thousands of readable long-tailed magpies interlocking into one immense arch, and two small abstract figures approaching from opposite ends.
- Style: contemporary Korean media art, sculptural light, translucent ink, subtle hanji and nacre texture.
- Palette: luminous indigo, moonlit ivory, restrained cyan, warm coral and soft violet.
- Avoid: cracks, rifts, lotus or mandala forms, decorative knots, cute mascots, game UI, generic portals, text, logos and watermarks.

## Runtime invariants

1. The bridge must read as birds before it reads as particles.
2. The two figures must remain small enough that the collective action of the flock is the protagonist.
3. The Milky Way is luminous and vertical; it is never represented as a dark crack.
4. No symbol unrelated to the Chilseok story may be introduced.
5. The 44-second and 50-second frames must remain legible at thumbnail scale.
