What this site does and does not do

Kmagpie Tools applies published Korean law and official government rate tables and shows you the result. Each page also shows the formula and the article of law it came from, so you can check the working rather than take the number on trust.

What it does not do is give advice. The site owner is not a tax accountant, a lawyer, or a certified labour attorney, and nothing here is tax, legal, employment or investment advice for your particular situation. These are calculators built on general assumptions, not a consultation.

Why your real figure may differ

A calculator only knows what you type into it. Real amounts depend on things it cannot see.

Pay and severance depend on your employment contract, your company's work rules, which allowances count as ordinary wage, and how probation, leave of absence or injury periods are treated. Taxes depend on the assessed standard value of the property, whether you actually meet the conditions for a reduction, local ordinances, and how your household is counted. Social insurance depends on what your employer reported and when the annual reconciliation lands. Unemployment benefit depends on why you left your job and how the employment centre rules on your eligibility.

Laws and rates also change. This site tries to keep up, but there can be a gap between an amendment taking effect and it appearing here. The table below shows which version of the rules each tool is currently using.

If you are worried about how a complaint might affect your visa, call 1345 about the visa question and 1350 about the labour question. They are separate systems. Labour law in Korea protects you regardless of nationality or visa type, including if your status has lapsed.

Nothing you type is stored

All calculations run inside your own browser. Your salary, your dates and your working hours are never sent to a server, never stored and never logged. Close the page and the numbers are gone.

The same applies to the image and PDF tools: files are processed in browser memory rather than uploaded, so a photo of your face or an unsigned contract never leaves your device.
